## Brand & Style
This design system crafts an upscale, tactile, and editorial urban exploration medium for the historic and leafy neighborhood of Aclimação in São Paulo. It bridges the romanticism of high-end print journalism with the civic identity of São Paulo's iconic deep blue street enamel signboards and the chromatic brilliance of Brazilian raw gems.

The aesthetic fuses **Editorial Precision** with **Gemological Lapidary Motifs**. Interfaces should breathe like heavyweight warm cotton paper, punctuated by deep mineral jewel tones to categorize neighborhood landmarks, artisanal bistros, cultural hubs, and wellness sanctuaries. The visual hierarchy maintains high editorial dignity: generous margins, sharp architectural baselines, subtle facet angles, and typographic distinction. It invites neighborhood residents and discerning urban flâneurs to discover Aclimação as an intimate jewel box hidden in the heart of São Paulo.

## Colors
The color architecture rests on an archival paper base paired with a mineral chromatic taxonomy. Every category in the district functions under its distinct precious stone signature:

- **Surface Base (Papel de Algodão):** `#F6F2EA` acts as the warm, tactile canvas across all standard backdrops. Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) is reserved strictly for interior facet cards and high-contrast street sign plates.
- **Text & Foundations (Ônix):** `#1C1A1F` anchors typographic weight with high-contrast legibility without the harshness of pure digital black.
- **Esmeralda (`#0F6B4F`):** Primary brand accent, environmental landmarks, public green spaces, and botanical destinations (e.g., Parque da Aclimação).
- **Safira Paulistana (`#1F3A93`):** Secondary brand tone inspired directly by São Paulo's municipal street signage. Governs institutional features, services, and neighborhood civic navigation.
- **Turmalina Paraíba (`#19C3B1`):** Tertiary focal accent for curated highlights, live status badges, interactive triggers, and rare gems.
- **Rubi (`#A31F34`):** Categorical accent for Gastronomy, bistros, bakeries, and nighttime dining.
- **Citrino (`#E9B824`):** Categorical accent for Health, body sanctuaries, pilates studios, and holistic wellbeing.
- **Ametista (`#6B3FA0`):** Categorical accent for Art galleries, architecture history, atelier routes, and cultural circuits.
- **Topázio Imperial (`#E0851E`):** Categorical accent for Weekend open-air events, markets, and seasonal gatherings.

Neutral tonal layering leverages subtle tints of natural stone: `#ECE6DB` for soft borders and `#DFD6C6` for dividers, ensuring visual rhythm without sterile grey tones.

## Typography
Typographic expression is rooted in the contrast between traditional Brazilian high-editorial print and utilitarian municipal signage:

- **Editorial Serifs (Playfair Display):** Conveys prestige, architectural heritage, and literary depth. Used for section intros, guide titles, spot highlights, and quote pulls. Headings make deliberate use of italicized variants for Portuguese terms of endearment or architectural nuances.
- **Architectural Sans (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Delivers immaculate clarity across long-form essays, descriptions, venue specifications, open hours, and data points. Its balanced geometry pairs harmoniously with Playfair Display's high stroke contrast.
- **Municipal Plaque Treatment:** Street labels and directional tags adopt uppercase `label-md` or `label-sm` weights in Plus Jakarta Sans with distinct tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`), reflecting the crisp, stencil-adjacent legibility of São Paulo street markers.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout emulates the rhythm of an archival folio or independent travel quarterly:

- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fixed canvas max-width of 1320px with generous margins (`3rem`) and gutters (`1.5rem`). Editorial feature articles utilize asymmetrical split layouts (7:5 or 8:4 column splits) to mimic print spreads where large gemological imagery offsets long-form text.
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid grid with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters. Category discovery ribbons become horizontal swipe carousels.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Fluid grid with compact `1.25rem` outer margins. Content groups prioritize vertical stacking with explicit `space-xl` visual breathing room between curated districts and editorial entries.
- **Component Rhythms:** Interior spacing adheres to disciplined multiples of `0.25rem`. Micro-metadata clusters (operating hours, distances) use `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while editorial container padding standardizes on `space-lg` and `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth
This design system avoids heavy synthetic drop shadows or glossy neumorphism. Depth is achieved via **Architectural Tonal Layers** and **Mineral Facet Insets**:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#F6F2EA` warm paper tone.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Blocks):** `#FFFFFF` surfaces sitting over the warm paper base, framed by ultra-fine hairline borders (`1px solid #ECE6DB`).
- **Level 2 (Active & Floating Layers):** Ambient, diffuse natural light shadow mimicking direct sunlight on textured cotton paper: `0px 8px 24px -4px rgba(28, 26, 31, 0.06), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(28, 26, 31, 0.03)`.
- **Lapidary Inset Faceting:** Featured editorial quotes and curated gems employ angled border chamfers (clipped corners) or dual-line borders (outer 1px `#1C1A1F`, inner 1px `#ECE6DB` with a 2px gap) referencing gem facets and heirloom stamps.
- **São Paulo Plaque Depth:** Street markers feature crisp 1.5px solid white framing inset by 2px within the deep `#1F3A93` plaque body, projecting zero shadow for an authentic baked-enamel appearance.

## Shapes
Shapes evoke both municipal craftsmanship and the geometry of gemstone cut styles (emerald cut, baguette, and brilliant facets):

- **Base Radius:** Elements use `roundedness: 1` (`0.25rem` / `4px`). This slight softening retains a crisp, structured, and architectural silhouette.
- **Facet Stamp Treatments:** Special badge seals, category chips, and spotlight containers employ clean corner chamfers (`clip-path: polygon(...)`) at 6px angles to directly reflect faceted stones.
- **Paulistano Street Sign Plate:** Rectangles with exactly `0.25rem` corner radius, surrounded by a crisp inner border set 3px inside the edge, recreating the municipal street identity of São Paulo.
- **Pill Exceptions:** Restricted solely to primary micro-pills containing status indicators (e.g., "Aberto Agora" with Turmalina Paraíba pulse dot).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Esmeralda Gem):** Solid `#0F6B4F` background with `#F6F2EA` text. 4px corner radius. Slight upward lift on hover with a subtle tone shift to `#0B523D`.
- **Secondary (Paulistano Enamel):** Background `#1F3A93`, text `#FFFFFF`, enclosed by a crisp 1px `#FFFFFF` inner inset stroke or white outline. Dedicated to navigation, mapping, and address actions.
- **Editorial Outline (Lapidary):** Transparent background, 1px solid `#1C1A1F` stroke, text `#1C1A1F`. Hover transitions to solid `#1C1A1F` with `#F6F2EA` text.

### Chips & Category Badges
- **Gemstone Classification Chips:** Subtle tinted background at 10% opacity of the stone color (e.g., Rubi `#A31F341A`), paired with a bold text label and a 1px solid border matching the corresponding gemstone hue.
- **Paulistano Street Plate Chip:** Solid `#1F3A93` fill, white uppercase lettering, 1px solid white inner inset border, used exclusively for street names (e.g., "RUA JUPURUCHITA", "AV. ACLIMAÇÃO").

### Cards & Place Profiles
- **Gem Profile Card:** White background `#FFFFFF`, hairline `#ECE6DB` border, 4px radius. Top banner or accent corner reflects the gemstone category. Image ratios maintain an editorial 4:3 or 16:10 ratio with deep duotone/natural contrast grade.
- **Editorial Curator Pick:** Features an angled facet corner (cutout top-right edge), a 2px Esmeralda accent rule, italicized Playfair Display sub-headings, and gemological coordinate badges.

### Lists & Directories
- **Gazetteer Table Rows:** Minimalist row layouts separated by `1px solid #ECE6DB` lines. Left side features street plate indicator or gem category icon; center holds title and editorial excerpt; right column displays operational status and walking time from Parque da Aclimação.

### Inputs & Search Fields
- **Directory Search Field:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, 1px solid `#DFD6C6` resting border, expanding to 1.5px solid `#0F6B4F` on focus. Placeholder text in `#1C1A1F` with 50% opacity. Clean geometry with no heavy shadows.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- **Checkboxes:** Squared with 2px radius, `#1C1A1F` stroke. On selection, filled with `#0F6B4F` featuring a crisp white geometric checkmark.
- **Radio Buttons:** Octagonal or diamond-faceted glyph appearance on active state, using `#0F6B4F` primary jewel tone.
