## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the quiet, verdant sophistication of Bairro da Aclimação in São Paulo: an architectural dialogue between mid-century residences, century-old trees surrounding the Parque da Aclimação, vibrant culinary pockets, and artisanal local culture. 

The aesthetic style merges **Refined Modern Editorial** with **Organic Tactility**:
- **Tone & Mood:** Cultivated, warm, observant, unhurried, and welcoming without pretense. It refrains from generic minimalism in favor of rich, bookish hierarchy and tactile neighborhood atmosphere.
- **Target Audience:** Cultured residents, urban explorers, local culinary patrons, and newcomers seeking curated community narratives, historical insights, architectural walking tours, and independent cultural venues.
- **Visual Balance:** Expansive negative space acts as canvas; rich botanical deep greens anchor authority; subtle ochre/amber accents evoke afternoon golden hour filtered through the park foliage; razor-thin hairlines replace heavy container boxes.

## Colors

The color palette is rooted in botanical tones, mineral nuances, and natural paper stocks:

- **Primary (`#1F4D3E`):** A deep, shade-dappled emerald green reminiscent of Brazilian fig trees and the lake perimeter. Used for main action anchors, header navigation accents, and primary typographic highlights.
- **Secondary (`#D4AF37`):** An antique warm amber/ochre evoking mid-afternoon light on park facades and brass architectural hardware. Used sparingly for curated tags, highlights, and verified neighborhood badges.
- **Tertiary (`#843B42`):** A muted pomegranate/jasper tone applied with strict moderation for arts & cultural markers, critical alerts, and select calendar highlights.
- **Neutral Surface & Base:**
  - Main Canvas (`#FBF9F5`): A warm, tactile limestone off-white that prevents screen glare and evokes archival print media.
  - Surface Raised / Card Container (`#F5F2EB`): A parchment shade providing gentle distinction without hard boundaries.
  - Borders & Hairlines (`#E6E1D7`): Whisper-soft organic lines.
- **Typography & Dark Neutrals:**
  - Ink Charcoal (`#1C1E1D`): Highest contrast, deep velvety graphite for titles and primary reading text.
  - Slate Graphite (`#4D5350`): For captions, metadata, and supporting editorial deck-lines.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial pace:

- **Headlines (`Newsreader`):** An expressive serif with transitional curves and literary warmth. Its optical styling lends an immediate sense of cultural authority, reminiscent of fine broadsheets and literary journals. Italic variants are reserved for pull quotes, author notes, and culinary venue descriptions.
- **Body and Labels (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** A modern geometric sans with warm, human terminals. Provides crystal-clear legibility across street maps, directory listings, venue opening hours, and mobile interfaces.
- **Editorial Conventions:**
  - Running text maintains generous line heights (`1.6x` to `1.7x`) to accommodate Portuguese diacritics comfortably.
  - Section overhead labels (`label-sm`) are rendered in small uppercase with a `0.06em` letter spacing to introduce architectural discipline above lyrical headlines.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout balances structural clarity with airy, magazine-style rhythm:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12-column grid with `margin: 3.5rem` and `gutter: 1.5rem`. Editorial long-form stories center inside an 8-column reading well, while photographic galleries and interactive neighborhood guides span the full 12 columns.
- **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8-column grid with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Asymmetrical splits collapse into balanced 4+4 layouts.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid with `margin: 1.25rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Visual margins ensure photography bleeds edge-to-edge when presenting immersive street scenes or cafe interiors, framed only by subtle inner padded captions.
- **Spacing Rhythm:** Generous vertical intervals (`space-xl` and above) are used between distinct editorial narratives to encourage relaxed pacing.

## Elevation & Depth

Rather than relying on heavy digital drop-shadows, this design system achieves depth through paper-like layering, tonal shifts, and atmospheric light:

- **Tonal Layering:** The primary canvas sits at `#FBF9F5`. Cards, drawer sheets, and interactive modules elevate by resting on `#F5F2EB` or pure white `#FFFFFF` with a 1px border colored `#E6E1D7`.
- **Atmospheric Shadows:** For floating search bars, filter bars, and modal overlays, shadows are extremely diffused, low-opacity, and tinted with the primary hue:
  - `elevation-low`: `0 2px 8px rgba(31, 77, 62, 0.04)`
  - `elevation-high`: `0 16px 36px rgba(31, 77, 62, 0.08), 0 2px 6px rgba(28, 30, 29, 0.03)`
- **Glass / Park Mist Overlays:** Sticky navigation headers use an 85% translucent `#FBF9F5` wash with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a subtle `border-bottom: 1px solid rgba(230, 225, 215, 0.8)` to let rich photography softly scroll beneath.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy is inspired by cut stone, tailored paper edges, and botanical curves:

- **Level 1 (Soft):** 
  - Standard interactive elements (buttons, text inputs, chips) carry a subtle `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius.
  - Image containers, cards, and editorial plaques use `0.5rem` (8px).
  - Modal sheets and primary dialog containers use `0.75rem` (12px).
- **Geometric Restraint:** Corners avoid heavy pill shapes to maintain an architectural, curated feel. The restraint mirrors modern Brazilian mid-century woodwork—softened at the point of touch, yet crisp in silhouette.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1F4D3E`, text `#FBF9F5`, border radius `4px`, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Hover transitions to `#2A6351` with slight upward translation.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, 1px solid border `#1F4D3E`, text `#1F4D3E`. Hover fills with a delicate 6% tint of `#1F4D3E`.
- **Editorial Text Link:** Pure typography with an offset, hairline underline in `#D4AF37`, shifting to full primary emerald on hover.

### Chips & Filter Tags
- Compact height (`32px`), radius `4px`, set in `label-sm`.
- Inactive: Background `#F5F2EB`, text `#4D5350`, border `1px solid #E6E1D7`.
- Active: Background `#1F4D3E`, text `#FBF9F5`, border `1px solid #1F4D3E`.

### Cards & Guide Modules
- **Editorial Guide Card:** Background `#FFFFFF` or `#F5F2EB`, border `1px solid #E6E1D7`, radius `8px`. Image ratios set to 4:3 or 16:10 with delicate inner bevels. Typography features a category label in amber or tertiary plum, followed by a Newsreader title and a 2-line summary.
- **Spotlight / Quote Card:** Borderless with an left vertical rule (`2px solid #D4AF37`), italic Newsreader text, and ample horizontal padding (`space-lg`).

### Input Fields & Search
- Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #D8D2C5`, radius `4px`, height `44px`. Focus state replaces border with `#1F4D3E` and adds a soft 2px focus ring (`rgba(31, 77, 62, 0.15)`).

### Selection Controls
- Checkboxes and radios have an unfilled state using a 1.5px border `#4D5350`. Checked state smoothly fills with `#1F4D3E` holding a crisp off-white checkmark.

### Specialized Components
- **Neighborhood Index / Map Pins:** Circular mineral markers with primary emerald borders and amber anchor dots.
- **Audio / Walk Tour Bar:** Minimalist horizontal pill hovering at the bottom edge with duration tickers and playback controls in polished deep green and brass.
